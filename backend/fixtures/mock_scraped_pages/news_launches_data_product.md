# Launches new data product for finance teams

*Press release*

The company today announced a new data product aimed at finance and operations teams. The product helps teams ship analytics on top of their transactional data without standing up additional infrastructure.

## What the product offers

- Pre-built data models for spend, payments, and ledgers
- Streaming ingestion built on Kafka
- A serverless query layer over Snowflake
- Audit trails suitable for compliance review

## Background

The launch follows a year of investment in data platform reliability and a public migration to Snowflake described in earlier engineering posts.
