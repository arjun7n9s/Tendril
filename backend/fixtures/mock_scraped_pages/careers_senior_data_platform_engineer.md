# Senior Data Platform Engineer

**Team:** Data Platform
**Location:** Remote (US) / New York, NY

We are scaling our data platform to support growing fintech workloads with strict compliance requirements. We are looking for an experienced engineer to own the reliability and performance of our streaming and analytics pipelines.

## What you will do

- Operate Kafka clusters in production at scale
- Improve Snowflake query performance and cost
- Build observability for data pipelines (dbt, Airflow)
- Partner with platform teams on reliability, SLOs, and incident response
- Drive a current migration from legacy ETL to a modern dbt + Snowflake stack

## What we look for

- 5+ years of production experience with Kafka and Snowflake
- Strong background in data quality, lineage, and observability
- Experience with regulated environments (SOC 2, PCI, fintech compliance)
- Comfort with Airflow, dbt, Python, and infrastructure-as-code

## About the team

The data platform team owns the systems that move and store every business-critical event in the company. We are investing heavily in reliability tooling and platform modernization through 2026.
